These pages are for the people who run Digital Collections Explorer: the person at a gallery, library, archive or museum who sets up a site, and the developer who extends it.

The [README](https://github.com/hinxcode/digital-collections-explorer#readme) still covers installing locally with Python, indexing large or remote collections, running with Docker, describing a collection to visitors, the limits that protect a public site, and deploying to a server or to AWS. Topics move here as they are written up in more depth.

## Deploying

- [Under a path on an existing website](/docs/deploy/under-a-path/): serve a site at `https://library.example.org/maps/` rather than on a domain of its own.
